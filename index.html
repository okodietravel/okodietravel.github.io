Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you get in touch.

Which countries do you help with scholarships?

We assist students with scholarships in the USA, the UK, Europe and China.

Can you guarantee a scholarship or a visa?

No consultant can honestly guarantee that. Decisions are made by universities, scholarship bodies and embassies. Our job is to help you prepare a complete, well-presented application.

How long does a visa process take?

It depends on the country, the type of visa and current processing times. Send us an enquiry and we will explain what to expect for your case.

What documents will I need?

Requirements differ by country and service. Tell us what you are applying for and we will let you know exactly what to prepare.

How much do your services cost?

Fees depend on the service you need. Send an enquiry and we will give you a clear answer before you commit to anything.
